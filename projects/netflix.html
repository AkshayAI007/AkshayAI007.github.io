THE PROBLEM

A single accuracy score hid a genre echo chamber.

Collaborative filtering needs watch history the catalogue doesn't have on day one. And optimising for raw accuracy produced a model that just kept recommending the same few genres back — technically precise, practically useless. The catalogue of 8,807 titles also needed recommendations fast enough to serve inline.

THE APPROACH

Benchmark eleven, then hybridise the best.

Each title's description, genres and cast become a document — vectorised with TF-IDF, reduced with NMF and LDA, and segmented with K-Means. Eleven candidate models were benchmarked across the full catalogue on Precision@10 and content diversity; the winning hybrid re-ranker was tuned to trade a sliver of precision for 43% more diverse recommendations, with cold-start fallback for brand-new titles and per-recommendation explanations.
