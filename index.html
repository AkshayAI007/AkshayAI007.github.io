CASE STUDY 01 / VOICE AI · MULTI-AGENT · ENTERPRISE AUTOMATION

From a two-day service queue to three minutes.

A production Voice AI architecture that verifies callers, understands service intent, enforces compliance and creates actionable records without leaving the conversation.

99.9%QUEUE LATENCY REDUCTION
87.5%FASTER FULFILMENT
24/7AUTONOMOUS AVAILABILITY

THE CONTEXT

Two days, for a phone call.

Inbound service requests were entering a manual queue. Customers waited up to two days before work even began, while identity checks, information capture and ticket creation crossed multiple systems and teams.

THE CONSTRAINT

Real-time doesn't forgive mistakes.

The system needed real-time latency, resilient handoffs, PII protection, PCI/HIPAA-aware guardrails and enough observability for enterprise production support.

THE ARCHITECTURE

Five agents, one job each.

01CALLER
02VERIFICATION
03CARE AGENT
04COMPLIANCE
05SALESFORCE

KEY DECISIONS

Verification comes before the conversation does.

  1. ✓01

    Separated verification, service fulfilment and compliance into specialised agents with explicit ownership.

  2. ✓02

    Integrated LiveKit and Twilio for real-time voice orchestration, interruption handling and call routing.

  3. ✓03

    Used URL lookups and OTP validation before exposing protected account context.

  4. ✓04

    Converted validated intent into structured Salesforce tickets through controlled tool calls.

THE OUTCOME

The autonomous workflow reduced queue latency from two days to three minutes and shortened overall fulfilment time by 87.5%, while maintaining a clear path for escalation and production handover.

CASE STUDY 02 / AGENTIC AI · B2B SALES INTELLIGENCE

A prospect view assembled by agents, not tabs.

A ReAct-style system that coordinates structured business retrieval, contextual interpretation and current web intelligence to give sales teams a decision-ready prospect narrative.

MONTHSBEFORE
DAYSAFTER
03SPECIALISED AGENTS

THE CONTEXT

Research that took longer than the sale.

Representatives were manually connecting internal business data, scattered prospect information and current market signals before they could form a useful pre-sales view.

THE CONSTRAINT

Fresh isn't the same as trustworthy.

Every insight needed traceable evidence, stable orchestration and concise output without allowing live-web noise to overwrite trusted internal context.

THE ARCHITECTURE

The web gets a vote, never the final word.

01USER FILTER
02SEARCH AGENT
03KNOWLEDGE
04WEB AGENT
05SWOT + GAPS

KEY DECISIONS

Inspectable beats impressive.

  1. ✓01

    Assigned discovery, interpretation and live enrichment to distinct agents instead of one oversized prompt.

  2. ✓02

    Used explicit routing and ReAct-style tool selection to keep actions inspectable.

  3. ✓03

    Prioritised trusted business context while using the web only to enrich time-sensitive claims.

  4. ✓04

    Structured the final output around SWOT, opportunity gaps and next-best sales actions.

THE OUTCOME

Personalised prospect intelligence compressed lead research and conversion preparation from months to days while giving representatives a more complete, consistent basis for action.

CASE STUDY 03 / LLMOPS · EVALUATION · PRODUCTION HANDOVER

Making model choice an engineering decision.

A repeatable evaluation approach balancing response quality, latency, cost and tool-use reliability instead of selecting models by leaderboard position or one impressive prompt.

04EVALUATION AXES
TRACEEVERY TOOL CALL
HUMANQUALITY REVIEW

THE CONTEXT

Confident answers, unreliable systems.

Model behaviour varied across realistic conversations, tool sequences and production load. A response could sound excellent while being too slow, too expensive or unreliable when action was required.

THE CONSTRAINT

Sounding right and being right are different problems.

The evaluation needed to reflect business-critical scenarios, capture regressions and remain understandable to both engineering and product stakeholders.

THE ARCHITECTURE

Every score traces back to a transcript.

01TEST SET
02MODEL RUN
03TOOL TRACE
04SCORING
05DECISION

KEY DECISIONS

Benchmarks don't catch what production does.

  1. ✓01

    Created representative scenario sets instead of relying only on generic benchmark questions.

  2. ✓02

    Measured semantic quality alongside latency, token cost and tool completion.

  3. ✓03

    Separated deterministic checks from rubric-based LLM and human review.

  4. ✓04

    Used traces and failure categories to guide production handover and future regression testing.

THE OUTCOME

Model selection became a transparent trade-off supported by scenario evidence, while handover teams received clearer failure modes, monitoring priorities and operational expectations.
