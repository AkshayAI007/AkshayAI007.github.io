<Column kicker="THE PROBLEM" title="Trip duration depends on more than distance.">
Two trips that cover the same straight-line distance can take very different amounts of time depending on the hour of day, the day of the week, and where in the city they start and end. A model that only sees raw coordinates and a timestamp has no way to capture that — the signal has to be engineered in.
</Column>
<Column kicker="THE APPROACH" title="Make the features carry the city's structure.">
The pipeline derives haversine distance, pickup hour and day-of-week, and location-based groupings from the raw pickup/drop-off coordinates and timestamps, then compares regression models on top of that engineered feature set — treating feature design, not model selection, as the main lever on accuracy.
</Column>
