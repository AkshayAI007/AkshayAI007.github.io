  title: Three agents. Five checkpoints.
  stops:
  - label: CALLER
    detail: Inbound call over Twilio SIP, streamed through LiveKit for real-time turn-taking and interruption handling.
  - label: VERIFICATION

  - label: CARE AGENT
    detail: Understands the service request and gathers what fulfilment needs, in natural conversation.

  - label: SALESFORCE
    detail: Validated intent becomes a structured ticket through a controlled tool call — no re-keying.
decisions:
  title: Verification comes before the conversation does.
  items:
  - title: Split verification, care and compliance into three agents.
    insteadOf: One voice agent carrying every policy in a single prompt.
    because: Compliance rules stop competing with conversation flow for the same context, and each agent can be tested on its own.
    tradeoff: More handoffs to engineer — each one a latency and state-transfer risk.
  - title: Prove identity before any account context is exposed.
    insteadOf: Verifying lazily, once the caller asks for something sensitive.
    because: PII never enters the model’s context for an unverified caller, which is what HIPAA and PCI DSS alignment demands.
    tradeoff: A few seconds of friction at the start of every call.
  - title: Run the whole exchange in real time on LiveKit and Twilio SIP.
    insteadOf: Capturing the request and calling back once it was processed.
    because: Resolving the request on the first call is what removes the queue entirely.
    tradeoff: A strict latency budget that constrains every model and tool choice.
  - title: File tickets through a controlled, structured tool call.
    insteadOf: A free-text call summary for a human to re-key.
    because: The ticket is actionable the moment the call ends, and every field is validated.
    tradeoff: Schemas must be kept in sync with Salesforce, with a fallback when intent is ambiguous.

<Story kicker="THE CONTEXT" title="Two days, for a phone call.">
Inbound service requests entered a manual queue. Customers waited up to two days before work began, while identity checks, information capture and ticket creation crossed several systems and teams.
</Story>
<Story kicker="THE CONSTRAINT" title="Real-time doesn’t forgive mistakes.">
The system had to hold a live conversation, hand off between agents without losing state, keep PII out of reach until identity was proven, and stay observable enough for enterprise production support.
</Story>
